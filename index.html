Enterprise O&M & AMC Services

End-to-end solar engineering solutions designed to protect assets, ensure high availability, and maximize plant performance ratio (PR).

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled rigorous inspections, thermal imaging, and mechanical testing to prevent equipment degradation and eliminate unscheduled downtime.

Solar Module Cleaning

Automated and manual high-efficiency module cleaning protocols removing dust, soiling, and bird droppings to sustain maximum irradiance capture.

24×7 SCADA Monitoring

Real-time continuous plant monitoring from our centralized operations desk to detect string losses, inverter trips, and grid anomalies instantly.

Emergency Breakdown Support

Rapid dispatch engineering teams equipped with diagnostic tools to resolve critical inverter, transformer, and HT breaker faults swiftly.

Inverter & Transformer AMC

Comprehensive OEM-compliant maintenance for central/string inverters, oil/dry transformers, VCBs, and HT power distribution panels.

Performance Ratio (PR) Analysis

Monthly deep-dive generation auditing, degradation modeling, and yield optimization reports delivered to asset owners.
